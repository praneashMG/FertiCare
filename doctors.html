Our Multidisciplinary Approach

At FertiCare, you are not just seeing one doctor. You are supported by an entire ecosystem of fertility professionals working in unison.

Physicians

Design and oversee your personalized clinical protocols and perform all retrievals and transfers.

Embryologists

Handle fertilization, vitrification, and carefully monitor embryo development in the lab.

Fertility Nurses

Your daily point of contact, guiding you through medication injections and cycle timing.

Wellness Counselors

Provide emotional support, nutritional advice, and mental health counseling throughout your journey.
