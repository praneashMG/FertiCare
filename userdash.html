Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I miss a medication dose?

Call our patient care line immediately. Do not double up on doses without speaking to your nurse first.

Can I exercise during stimulation?

Gentle walking and light stretching are fine, but avoid high-impact activities or twisting motions as your ovaries enlarge.

How long does the egg retrieval take?

The procedure itself takes about 20–30 minutes. You'll rest in recovery for 1–2 hours before going home.

When will I know my lab results?

Most results are uploaded to your dashboard within 24 hours. Your care team will message you directly if anything needs attention.
