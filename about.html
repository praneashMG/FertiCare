Our Story of Creating Miracles

FertiCare Clinic was founded on a simple but powerful belief: everyone who dreams of becoming a parent deserves compassionate, world-class medical support to make it happen.

Meet Our Experts Visit Our Clinic

Guided by Purpose, Driven by Hope

Our foundation is built on scientific excellence and unwavering empathy for our patients.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, cutting-edge fertility treatments in a supportive environment. We strive to demystify the IVF process and empower our patients with knowledge, transparent success rates, and personalized medical care.

Our Vision

To be the global benchmark in reproductive medicine and holistic fertility care. We envision a future where infertility is universally treatable, and every aspiring parent receives the support they need to build their family.

A Legacy of Fertility Excellence

From our humble beginnings to becoming a leading IVF center, our journey is defined by the families we've helped create.

2010

FertiCare is Founded

Opened our doors with a core team of 3 specialists, dedicating ourselves to affordable IUI and fertility diagnostics.

2015

Advanced Embryology Lab

Launched our state-of-the-art Class 100 cleanroom lab, introducing high-success IVF and ICSI procedures.

2020

1000+ Families Grown

Celebrated a major milestone, officially welcoming over 1,000 babies into the world through our treatments.

Today

Pioneers in Genetic Screening

Integrating AI and advanced PGT-A testing to offer some of the highest embryo transfer success rates in the region.
