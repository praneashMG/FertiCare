Our Primary Treatments

Evidence-based reproductive medicine designed to maximize your chances of a successful, healthy conception.

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

Eggs are retrieved from the ovaries and fertilized by sperm in our advanced laboratory, then carefully transferred into the uterus.

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

A minimally invasive procedure where specially washed and concentrated sperm is placed directly into the uterus during ovulation.

Fertility Preservation

Advanced egg and sperm freezing (vitrification) allows you to pause your biological clock and preserve your reproductive options for the future.

Advanced Reproductive Technology

Our Class 100 cleanroom embryology lab is equipped with cutting-edge tools to overcome severe male and female infertility factors.

ICSI

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection. A single healthy sperm is injected directly into an egg, overcoming severe male infertility issues.

PGT-A & PGT-M

Preimplantation Genetic Testing screens embryos for chromosomal abnormalities and specific genetic diseases prior to transfer.

Assisted Hatching

A highly specialized laser technique used to thin the embryo's outer shell, significantly improving implantation rates.

Specialized Patient Pathways

We categorize our care plans to provide targeted, highly specific treatment protocols depending on your medical background.

Female Factor

Targeted treatments for PCOS, Endometriosis, tubal blockages, and diminished ovarian reserve (DOR).

Male Factor

Advanced andrology services including TESA/PESA extraction, DNA fragmentation testing, and ICSI.

Recurrent Loss

Specialized investigative protocols and immune therapies for patients experiencing recurrent miscarriages.
