Advanced Reproductive Technology

Our Class 100 cleanroom embryology lab is equipped with cutting-edge tools to overcome severe male and female infertility factors.

ICSI

Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection. A single healthy sperm is injected directly into an egg, overcoming severe male infertility issues.

PGT-A & PGT-M

Preimplantation Genetic Testing screens embryos for chromosomal abnormalities and specific genetic diseases prior to transfer.

Assisted Hatching

A highly specialized laser technique used to thin the embryo's outer shell, significantly improving implantation rates.

Specialized Patient Pathways

We categorize our care plans to provide targeted, highly specific treatment protocols depending on your medical background.

Female Factor

Male Factor

Recurrent Loss

Clinical Accreditation & Lab Safety

Behind the Success: Our Certified Cleanroom Lab

Embryo development requires microscopic precision in an environment that faithfully mimics the maternal womb. Our ISO Class 5 cleanroom embryology suite incorporates the strictest clinical benchmarks in modern reproductive science.

VOC & HEPA Air Lock

Continuous positive pressure filtration removing volatile compounds and airborne particulates.

RFID Electronic Witnessing

Automated barcoding ensures 100% zero-error identification across every egg, sperm, and embryo sample.

Tri-Gas Benchtop Incubators

Individual low-oxygen culture chambers preventing environmental shock and maximizing blastocyst yields.

CAP & SART Accredited

Consistently audited laboratory benchmarks exceeding national clinical average success benchmarks.

Precision Embryology Workstation

Dual-operator stereo microscopes with climate-controlled heated working stages.
